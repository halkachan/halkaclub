Retakes

There is no limit on the number of retakes for a commission from HALKA.

  • “Could you make this part a little more like that?”
  • “Could you bring the vocals a bit further forward?”
  • “Could you change the sound here?”
  • “Could you adjust the character’s expression a little?”

If anything bothers you, please do not hesitate to tell me.
I will adjust it as many times as it takes, at no extra charge, until you are happy with it.

I also send progress updates and check in with you while I work, so please bring up anything you notice as we go.

Delivery Time

Because I work on commissions alongside my own activities, I do not accept rush jobs such as within 24 hours or within a few days.
For any commission, please allow at least about one week.

The actual delivery time varies depending on:

  • What you are commissioning
  • The length and difficulty of the song
  • The amount of work involved
  • The retakes requested
  • How many commissions I currently have
  • HALKA’s own activity schedule

Plans that involve several stages in particular, such as:

  • Vocal Cover Full Package
  • Original Song Production
  • Original Song Full Package

may take anywhere from a few weeks to a month or more, depending on the song.

I will give you a rough delivery date together with the quote, before production starts.

If you already have a release date in mind, such as “I want to post it on this day”, please tell me when you get in touch.
If there is not enough time to produce the work properly, I may have to decline.

How It Works

  1. 1 Get in touch by email or on X / Twitter
  2. 2 We go over the details of your request
  3. 3 I send the quote and the payment details
  4. 4 Production, review and revisions
  5. 5 Delivery of the finished files

Payment

From within Japan: Bank transfer or PayPal
From outside Japan: PayPal Invoice

I will confirm the final quote and the payment method
after going over the details of your request.

Production starts once the payment has been confirmed.
For larger commissions, paying in installments can be arranged.

All prices are charged in Japanese yen (JPY).
